## 1.1.0

- Saves the game's own counts of your deaths, kills, gold, flights and more, and when you learn to ride and train each profession rank, for your climb in the guide.

## 1.0.0

- First release: saves each character's finished quests, race, class, talents, level, reputation and profession skills for the Forever Levels guide.
- Saves each character's quest log, what it saw happen (quests picked up and handed in, bosses killed), its play times and time played, hearthstone, gold, riding, what it holds, flight paths and dungeon boss kills.
- Shows your XP an hour on the XP bar's tooltip, a line in chat at each level, and your biggest crits, each with its own checkbox in Options > AddOns.
